For teachers: how this works

Built for "why am I learning this?"

Each tool follows the same four steps: the problem (a real case with real numbers), gut vs. math (students lock in a gut answer, then the math takes a turn and the tool shows the difference in real terms), your day runs on this (where modern life depends on this exact skill) and review (real-world and test-style questions with worked solutions).

Why are we learning this? cards

Choose your course under Browse, then open a unit. The unit page starts with a Why are we learning this? card: an opening question, the problems this unit solves and the everyday technology that depends on it. Press Project this on the first day of the unit.

Three levels of detail

Big picture gives the main idea in everyday words. Closer look names the math and adds variables. Deep dive adds the formulas, derivations and harder questions. Any student can choose any level.

Put a tool in Canvas

Open any tool and use Embed this tool in Canvas at the bottom. Choose where it starts, copy the code and paste it into a Canvas page.

Privacy

Everything runs in the browser. There are no logins, and no student data leaves the device. An anonymous page counter (no cookies, no names) records which tools are opened and whether the visit came through a school device.
